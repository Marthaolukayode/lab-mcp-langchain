
Company AI Usage Policy

1. Employees may use approved AI tools for research, drafting, and summarizing documents.

2. Confidential customer information must not be entered into public AI tools.

3. All AI-generated reports must be reviewed by a human before being shared externally.

4. Employees must report suspected AI security incidents to the IT department within 24 hours.

5. The IT department is responsible for approving new AI tools.
